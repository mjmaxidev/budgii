## Description

Budgii makes household budgeting simple, clear and collaborative.

See where your money is going, plan what comes next and make better decisions together. Whether you are managing your own budget or sharing expenses with family, Budgii keeps the important details in one calm, easy-to-use place.

Track expenses as they happen, organise spending by category, and review your progress with clear reports. Scan receipts to extract purchase details, then check and edit everything before it is saved. Set budgets, monitor spending, manage recurring transactions and keep a practical shopping list ready for your next trip.

Budgii also helps households work together with shared members, roles and invitations. Keep everyone on the same page without losing sight of the bigger picture.

With Budgii, you can:

- Track expenses, income and recurring transactions
- Set monthly budgets and spending alerts
- Scan receipts and review extracted items
- Explore reports and category breakdowns
- Share a household budget with family members
- Organise shopping lists and deal ideas
- Protect access with secure sign-in and an optional app PIN

Smart budgets. Better futures.

## TestFlight Build Information

### What to Test — English (Australia)

Please test the complete household budgeting flow:

- Sign in with the supplied review account and confirm the Home dashboard loads.
- Review the monthly budget, spending progress and category totals.
- Add a manual expense and confirm it appears in Transactions and Reports.
- Scan or upload a receipt, review the extracted merchant, total and items, then confirm it.
- Open Reports and Spending Alerts to check the budget summaries and alert settings.
- Review Shopping List, Family Members and Settings.
- Sign out and sign back in to confirm the session and household data are restored.

Please also report any issues with data syncing, receipt extraction, navigation, layout, or sign-in. Receipt extraction results are suggestions and should be reviewed before confirmation.
